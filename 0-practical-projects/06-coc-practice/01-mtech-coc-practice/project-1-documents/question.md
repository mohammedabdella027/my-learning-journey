## Project Scenario and Background

Assume that you have received a request for a comprehensive technological solution from MTech Import and Export Company. The company is engaged in exporting various items (such as mining products, livestock, coffee, and others) and importing various items (such as machinery, food and beverages, cosmetics, and others).

To execute all operational activities efficiently, the company is structured into four primary functional divisions:

| Division | Responsibility and scope |
|---|---|
| Stock Division | Responsible for stock management, including item registration, inventory tracking, and data operations. |
| Finance Division | Responsible for handling and managing all financial activities, transactions, and reporting. |
| Customer Relation Division | Manages continuous interaction and relationship management between the company and its clients. |
| Human Resource Division | Responsible for overall workforce administration, staff management, and personnel operations. |

## Project 1 Tasks

### Task 1: Prepare Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
**Instruction:** List required materials, hardware, software, and technical professionals along with estimated costs in an appropriate standard format.

### Task 2: Prepare Activities Plan
**Instruction:** Outline the full list of project activities to be performed, assigning each activity to the responsible technical professional in a structured format.

### Task 3: Prepare Work Time Schedule
**Instruction:** Develop a clear, structured time schedule for all work items using weeks as the standard time unit.

### Task 4: Prepare Database and System Architecture
**Instruction:** Design and draw the database architecture using an Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD) and standard web architecture design mechanisms.

### Task 5: Prepare Scalability and Standardization Plan
**Instruction:** Formulate a quality assurance and standardization plan to ensure future scalability and reliability.

### Task 6: Prepare Maintenance Procedures and Recovery Plan
**Instruction:** Detail operational maintenance procedures and establish a robust disaster recovery plan to ensure system availability and data protection.
